# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

## GET IT DONE

**Cooperative Gig Services Platform for Household & Community Services**

**Problem Statement ID:** 26089
**Organization:** Ministry of Cooperation

**Category:** Software
**Theme:** Smart Automation

---

# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

**GET IT DONE** is a cooperative-owned digital platform that connects households, institutions, and communities with verified local skilled workers.

The platform allows customers to easily find and book workers such as electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, cleaners, caregivers, drivers, gardeners, and technicians.

At the same time, it helps cooperative workers receive more opportunities, earn fairly, and access worker welfare services.

The system also uses AI to predict service demand and help cooperative administrators allocate workers efficiently.

---

## 1.2 Problem Statement

Many Labour Cooperative Federations and Labour Cooperative Societies have a large number of skilled workers, but there is no organized digital system to connect them with people who need their services.

Private service platforms currently dominate this market. Cooperative workers may have the required skills and local experience but still remain underutilized.

Customers also face difficulties in finding:

* Trusted workers
* Skilled professionals
* Available workers nearby
* Emergency services
* Transparent pricing
* Reliable service providers

**GET IT DONE** addresses these problems through one cooperative-owned platform.

---

# 2. Objectives

The main objectives of GET IT DONE are:

1. Connect customers with verified local workers.
2. Provide workers with more job opportunities.
3. Support fair and transparent earnings.
4. Make service booking simple and fast.
5. Match customers with suitable nearby workers.
6. Provide emergency and on-demand services.
7. Enable digital payments and invoices.
8. Build customer trust through ratings and verification.
9. Support worker welfare and insurance.
10. Use AI to forecast demand.
11. Help cooperatives manage their workforce efficiently.
12. Provide multilingual access for users.

# 9. Location-Based Matching

The system shall identify suitable workers based on:

* Distance
* Skill match
* Availability
* Certification
* Rating
* Current workload
* Service area

Example:

```text
Customer requires:
Emergency Plumbing

Available Workers:

Worker A
Distance: 1.2 km
Rating: 4.8
Availability: Yes

Worker B
Distance: 2.0 km
Rating: 4.9
Availability: Yes

Worker C
Distance: 3.1 km
Rating: 4.7
Availability: Yes
```

The system shall recommend the most suitable worker rather than simply selecting the closest worker.

---

# 10. Smart Worker Matching

The platform shall use a matching score.

Example:

```text
Skill Match        30%
Distance           20%
Availability       15%
Certification      15%
Rating             10%
Workload Balance   10%
```

This prevents the same workers from receiving all available jobs.

# 31. Business Rules

1. Only verified workers can receive regular service bookings.
2. Workers must have the required skill for a service.
3. Emergency jobs should prioritize available qualified workers.
4. Workers should not be overloaded when other suitable workers are available.
5. Payments must be associated with a valid booking.
6. Reviews can only be submitted after a completed booking.
7. Administrators can suspend workers or customers for serious violations.
8. Worker earnings must be recorded transparently.
9. AI recommendations should be reviewable by authorized administrators.
10. Personal and payment information must be protected.

# 35. Conclusion

**GET IT DONE** provides a digital bridge between customers who need services and skilled workers belonging to Labour Cooperatives.

Instead of operating like a normal private gig platform, the system focuses on **cooperative ownership, worker welfare, fair opportunities, customer trust, and smart workforce management**.

With location-based matching, digital payments, verified workers, emergency booking, multilingual support, and AI-powered demand forecasting, GET IT DONE can create a scalable digital ecosystem for cooperative workers and the communities they serve.
